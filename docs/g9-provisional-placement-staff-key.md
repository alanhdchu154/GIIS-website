# G9 Provisional Placement Packet — Staff Scoring Key

Keep this key out of the student-facing packet. It is an admissions-review aid,
not an automatic admission, credit, grade, or transcript decision.

## English anchor points

- Central idea: the library tool-lending program reduced access barriers and
  became a community-sharing resource.
- Supporting details: tools cost too much for brief use; volunteers repaired
  tools; families exchanged gardening knowledge; seed demand created a waitlist.
- *Instead* contrasts the feared problems with the positive actual outcome.
- Reasonable inference: volunteers are engaged/community-minded and helped make
  the program sustainable; the response must cite the repair detail.
- Writing response: score claim/reasons (0–6), treatment of a concern and use
  of evidence (0–5), and organization/conventions (0–5). Do not penalize an
  unfamiliar cultural example when the reasoning is clear.

## Science anchor points

1. Metal cup is expected to transfer heat to the ice faster than foam. A fair
   test keeps ice size, room, cup size, placement, and timing constant; the
   response should identify observation/measurement, not simply repeat a
   prediction.
2. Less grass reduces food for rabbits, so rabbits likely decrease; foxes may
   later decrease or face less prey. Accept careful qualifications rather than
   a single deterministic claim.
3. Disagree: plants use sunlight energy to combine carbon dioxide and water to
   make sugars; soil supplies minerals/water but is not the source of all food.
   Award for a coherent causal explanation, not memorized vocabulary alone.

## Decision safeguards

- Recheck any result near a section threshold with a short oral walkthrough or
  alternate item before recommending a bridge.
- If language access plausibly limited a science/math response, distinguish
  subject knowledge from English-proficiency support needs.
- Record accommodations and any assistance that occurred. Do not convert a
  score to a transcript grade or a transfer-credit award.
